# AMO listing — en-US

## Name

Section Nav for ChatGPT

## Summary

Navigate headings in the current ChatGPT answer and save important sections locally.

## Description

Section Nav for ChatGPT adds a lightweight Section Rail beside the answer you are currently reading. It detects H1, H2, and H3 headings, follows your reading position, and lets you jump directly to a section without changing ChatGPT's original layout.

Save important sections with a star and reopen them from the Bookmark Drawer. Bookmark metadata stays in Firefox local extension storage and is separated by ChatGPT conversation. Nothing is uploaded to the developer or a third-party server.

Features:

- Navigate headings in the active ChatGPT answer.
- Track the active answer and current section while scrolling.
- Save and remove local section bookmarks.
- Recover bookmark targets after ChatGPT single-page navigation and lazy rendering.
- Adapt between full, compact, and minimal layouts.
- Follow ChatGPT light and dark themes.
- Respect reduced-motion and forced-colors accessibility settings.

The extension requests only the `storage` permission and page access to `https://chatgpt.com/*`. It contains no analytics, telemetry, remote code, advertisements, or paid features.

Section Nav for ChatGPT is an independent extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by OpenAI or Mozilla.
